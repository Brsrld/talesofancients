Privacy Policy

Tales of Ancients and Tales Uploader. Last updated: 25 October 2026.

1. Summary

Tales Uploader is used only by the channel's operator to post the channel's own videos to the channel's own accounts. It does not collect data from viewers or from any other person.

2. What Tales Uploader stores

  • The access tokens the platforms issue when the operator signs in to the channel's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts. They are kept in the operator's computer's encrypted keychain.
  • The basic profile of the channel's own account (display name and account ID), used to confirm the right account is connected.
  • The channel's own videos, titles and captions before they are posted.

3. What it does not do

  • It does not read, collect or store data about viewers, followers or other users.
  • It does not sell or share any data, and uses no advertising or tracking.

4. The website

This website has no sign-up, no forms, no cookies and no analytics.

5. Deleting data

The operator can revoke Tales Uploader's access at any time in the platform's settings (on TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services). The stored tokens are then removed from the keychain.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

7. Contact

Send a direct message to @talesofancients on TikTok or Instagram.
