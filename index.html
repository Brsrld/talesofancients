Tales of Ancients

Ancient tales & legends in moving engravings.

Tales of Ancients is a small independent channel of short vertical films. Each film retells an old story or legend from a published source in six short scenes, drawn in the style of classic engravings and animated.

The films are made with AI image and video models and are labelled as AI-generated.
